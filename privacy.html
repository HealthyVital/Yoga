Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026  ·  Yogi Khasha
This Privacy Policy describes how Yogi Khasha collects, uses, and discloses your information when you use our platform, and explains your privacy rights. By using Yogi Khasha you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.
§
Interpretation & Definitions

Words with initial capital letters have the meanings defined below. These definitions apply whether terms appear in singular or plural form.

Account
A unique account created for you to access our Service or parts of it.
Company
Refers to Yogi Khasha, the online wellness platform operated at yogikhasha.com. Also referred to as "We," "Us," or "Our."
Cookies
Small files placed on your device by a website containing browsing details and preferences.
Device
Any device that can access the Service such as a computer, mobile phone, or tablet.
Personal Data
Any information that relates to an identified or identifiable individual.
Service
Refers to the Yogi Khasha website and platform, accessible at yogikhasha.com.
Service Provider
Any third party that processes data on behalf of Yogi Khasha to facilitate, deliver, or analyze our Service (e.g., Stripe for payment processing).
Usage Data
Data collected automatically from your use of the Service, such as page visit duration, IP address, and browser type.
You
The individual accessing or using the Service, or the entity on whose behalf access is made.
1
Data We Collect
Personal Data

When you create an account or subscribe, we may collect:

  • Email address
  • First and last name
  • Payment information (processed and stored securely by Stripe — Yogi Khasha does not store full card numbers)
  • Subscription plan details and billing history
Usage Data

Usage data is collected automatically and may include your IP address, browser type and version, the pages you visit on our platform, time and date of visits, time spent on pages, and other diagnostic data.

Content Interaction Data

We may record which classes, series, or courses you access, and your practice activity (such as practice days logged) to provide features like the Badges progress system.

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to maintain your session and remember your preferences. Types include:

  • Necessary Cookies: Essential to provide core platform functionality.
  • Acceptance Cookies: Record whether you have consented to cookie use.
  • Functionality Cookies: Store preferences such as your login state and selected plan.
2
How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the Yogi Khasha platform and your membership.
  • To manage your account and process payments via Stripe.
  • To contact you regarding subscription renewals, platform updates, new content, or security notices.
  • To personalize your experience, including tracking practice progress for the Badges system.
  • To analyze usage patterns and improve the platform.
  • To enforce our Terms of Service and protect the rights and safety of our users.
Content Watermarking: Videos served to logged-in members are watermarked with account identity data (email address). This data is embedded visually in the video stream to deter and trace unauthorized sharing. By accessing premium video content, you consent to this use of your email address.
3
Sharing Your Data

We do not sell your personal data. We may share your information in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With third-party services that help us operate the platform, including Stripe (payment processing). These providers are bound by confidentiality obligations and may only use your data to deliver their services.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, court order, or governmental authority.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction.
  • With Your Consent: For any other purpose with your explicit consent.
5
Retention & Transfer of Data

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, provide your membership, and comply with legal obligations. When your account is closed, we will delete or anonymize your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain it by law.

Your information may be transferred to and processed on servers located outside your jurisdiction. Where this occurs, we take appropriate steps to ensure your data is handled securely and in accordance with this policy and applicable data protection laws.

6
Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights including:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Objection: Object to certain types of processing, including marketing.
  • Portability: Request a portable copy of your data in a machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at Yogi_Khasha@proton.me. We will respond within 30 days.

7
Children's Privacy

The Yogi Khasha platform is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately at Yogi_Khasha@proton.me and we will take steps to delete that information.

8
Security

We take the security of your data seriously. Payment information is processed exclusively through Stripe, a PCI-DSS compliant payment processor — Yogi Khasha never stores full card details. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9
Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the date at the top of this page. For material changes, we will notify active members by email where possible. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the platform after any update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
